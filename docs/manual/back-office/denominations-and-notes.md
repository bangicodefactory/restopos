# Denominations and kitchen notes

Two small lists the till reads every time it starts. Neither could be edited before, so both were
whatever the system was installed with.

## Coin and note denominations

**Denominations** lists the physical money your currency uses. The till reads it in two places:

- the **count sheet** at the end of a shift, where a cashier tallies how many of each note and coin
  are in the drawer;
- the **quick-tender keys** on the payment screen — the "20", "50" buttons a cashier presses instead
  of typing.

Add one with its name, its value and whether it is a note or a coin. The value is what the count
sheet multiplies a tally by, so it has to be the real face value.

If your venue trades in a currency the system did not ship with, this is the list to fill in first.
Until it is right, the count sheet is asking your staff to count notes that do not exist and missing
the ones that do.

Removing a denomination takes a key off the payment screen and a line off the count sheet. Nothing
else refers to it, so nothing else changes.

## Predefined kitchen notes

**Kitchen notes** are the notes staff pick from instead of typing: *no ice*, *allergy — nuts*, *well
done*. They go onto the ticket the kitchen reads.

Each note is attached in one of two places, and choosing the wrong one puts it in the wrong place on
the ticket:

- **On a line** — the note belongs to one item. *No ice* belongs to the drink.
- **On the order** — the note belongs to the whole table. *Table is in a hurry* is not about one dish.

A name can only be used once, so there is never a picker with two identical entries on it. Other
venues are free to use the same names as you.

Removing a note takes it off the picker. Notes already written on past orders are untouched — the
text is copied onto the order when it is chosen, so deleting *no ice* does not rewrite the history of
every drink that went out without ice.

## Who can change these

Both lists are register configuration, so changing them needs the same permission as the rest of the
register's setup.

## Which registers get them

Both lists are shared across the venue, and a register chooses which of them it uses on its own
settings page. The rule reads backwards from what most people expect — a note or denomination ticked
on a register belongs to *that* register and stops appearing on the others, while one ticked nowhere
appears everywhere. [What a register sends to the kitchen](register-preparation.md) explains it in
full, including why the note list is frozen while a session is open.
