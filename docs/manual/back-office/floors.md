# Dining rooms

A room is a floor plan: the main dining room, the terrace, the bar. Tables live on a room, and the
register's floor screen shows one room at a time.

## Add a room

**Floors → Add** and give it a name. It appears after the rooms you already have, so the order on the
till stays the one your staff are used to — set a position yourself if you want it somewhere else.

Then open it and place the tables. That is the same editor the till uses, so a manager standing in
the venue can rearrange the room from the register instead.

## Remove a room

**Remove**. Two things can stop you, and they are different.

**A room with open bills cannot be removed at all.** The till lists the tables that are still open so
you can go and close them. This is not caution for its own sake: a bill on a deleted room does not
disappear, it becomes unreachable — the floor screen cannot draw it and the order list filters it
out, so the money is still owed and nothing on any screen says where it is.

**A room with tables asks you to confirm.** The tables go with the room, and every table carries the
QR code printed on the card sitting on it. Re-creating the room later issues new codes, and every
printed card in that room stops working. Confirm and it goes.

An empty room is removed without a fuss.

## Who can change this

Adding or removing a room is a manager task, the same permission as rearranging one from the till.
