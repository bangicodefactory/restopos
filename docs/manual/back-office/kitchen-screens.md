# Kitchen screens

A kitchen screen is a station's board: the tickets it is responsible for, and the columns staff move
them through. A venue can run one, or one per section.

## Add a screen

**Kitchen screens → Add** and give it a name. Then open it to choose which product categories it
receives and how it behaves — how long a ticket may sit before it is flagged late, how long finished
tickets stay on screen, whether a new order makes a sound.

A new screen starts with three columns: **to do**, **in progress** and **ready**. Those are the
minimum a board needs to be usable — one to see work arrive, one to claim it, one to call it away.
Change them once the screen exists; the order of the columns is the order work moves through, so it
is worth setting up the way your kitchen actually runs.

The screen gets its own address when you create it. That address is what you open on the physical
display, and it is issued by the system rather than chosen — nobody can guess another screen's.

## Remove a screen

**Remove**. The till refuses while tickets are still on the board and says how many.

That is not caution for its own sake. Removing a screen removes its tickets, and the orders those
tickets came from still say the kitchen was told — food somebody is cooking would stop existing on
the only screen showing it, and nothing anywhere would say so.

Tickets that have been **served or cancelled** do not count. They are on the board only until the
retention time you set runs out.

Clear the board, or wait for it, and the screen goes — along with its columns.

## Who can change this

Adding or removing a kitchen screen is a manager task, the same permission as configuring one.
