# Self-order and table QR codes

**Back office → Registers → open a register → Self-order.** This is where you turn on ordering from a
customer's own phone, or from a kiosk, and where you print the QR codes that get stuck to tables.

## Print the QR codes for your tables

Open the **QR codes** tab. Every table this register serves is listed, each with its own code and its
table number printed above it. Choose a room if you have several, then **Print sheet**.

**Each code belongs to one table.** Scanning the one on table 6 opens an order for table 6 — that is
what makes the food arrive in the right place. The codes are not interchangeable, and they are not
guessable.

Until recently this page could not see your tables at all, and asked you to paste table tokens into a
box by hand. If you have QR cards printed that way, it is worth checking a few: a code copied into the
wrong row produces a card on table 6 that opens table 9's order, and nothing anywhere would have said
so.

**A code carries no expiry.** Print it once, stick it down, and it works until you rotate the token.

## Rotating the token

**Rotate** issues a new token for the whole venue. Every QR code already printed — every table card,
every poster — stops working immediately and must be reprinted.

Do it if a code has been photographed and shared somewhere it should not have been. Do not do it
casually: there is no partial rotation, and no way to undo it.

## Choose the language customers see

**Default language** decides what a customer is greeted in when they scan. This one control could not
be saved at all until recently — it rendered, you could change it, and it came back to the old value
after a reload. If you set your language once and found it had not stuck, that is why; setting it
again will work.

## Choose how customers pay

**Online payment method** is where a customer's money goes when they pay from their phone. Only your
own venue's payment methods are offered.

**Pay after** decides whether they settle each order as they place it, or accumulate a tab and pay at
the end. A tab only makes sense with table service — a counter or kiosk customer walks away.

## Branding

**Brand image** and the colours appear on the customer's phone or the kiosk screen. Upload the image
here; see [Images](images.md) for what the system accepts and why SVG files are refused.

## Custom links

Links you add appear on the customer's menu — an allergen page, a loyalty signup, your own website.
Only links belonging to your own venue can be attached, so a link from another business on the same
system cannot end up in front of your customers.
