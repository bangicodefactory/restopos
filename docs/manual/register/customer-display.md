# The screen facing the customer

The customer display shows the person in front of you what you are ringing up: each item as it goes
on, the running total, what they have paid and what is left, and a thank-you when the sale settles.
It shows nothing else — no prices you have not charged, no other tables, no way to touch the order.

## Putting one on

On the till, press **Customer display** in the top bar. A panel opens with one address on it, and
two ways to use it.

**A second monitor on this till.** Press **Open here**. A window opens; drag it onto the second
screen and make it full screen. Pressing the button again brings that window back to the front
rather than opening a second one.

**A separate device** — a small screen on the counter, a tablet on a stand. Press **Copy the
address**, send it to that device however you normally would, and open it there. It will start
following this till as soon as the page loads.

Nothing is typed in and nothing is paired: the address is the whole setup. Treat it the way you
would treat a key. Anyone who has it can watch that till's screen, so do not post it publicly.

## Which one to choose

The two are not the same, and the difference matters on a bad day.

A **second monitor on the till** does not use the network at all. If the venue's internet goes
down mid-service, that display keeps working exactly as before, because the till is talking to the
window next to it.

A **separate device** follows over the network. If the connection drops, that screen freezes on the
last thing it was shown until the connection comes back — the till keeps trading either way, and
nobody has to touch the display when it returns.

So: if the screen can be plugged into the till, plug it into the till.

## Its own look

A manager can set a background image for the display in the back office, under the register's
settings. It sits behind the order with the text darkened over it so the total stays readable from
across a counter. Leave it unset and the display is plain dark, which is what it has always been.

The venue's name is shown at the top and on the idle screen between sales.

## When it looks wrong

**The screen is blank or still says "Welcome" while you are ringing items up.** It is not following
this till. Open the panel again and re-open the address on that device — most often it is a display
left over from a different till, or a page that was open before the till finished starting up.

**A separate device is one item behind and stays there.** Its connection has dropped. It catches up
on its own when the network returns; there is nothing to press.

**Nothing you can do on the display affects the sale.** That is deliberate. It has no buttons, it
cannot open an order, and a customer leaning on it changes nothing.
